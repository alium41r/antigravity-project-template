# Technical Spikes & Feasibility

Store findings from technical spike investigations, experimental proofs of concept (PoCs), and performance benchmarks here.

## Documentation Guidelines
- **Goal**: What technical uncertainty were we trying to resolve?
- **Options Evaluated**: Which libraries, patterns, or tools were tested?
- **Findings**: What were the trade-offs, performance numbers, or blockers?
- **Recommendation**: What is the selected approach and why?
