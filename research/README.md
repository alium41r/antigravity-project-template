# Research

This directory contains analytical and empirical findings that inform both product strategy and technical execution.

## 📂 Subdirectories

- **`market/`**: Market sizing, industry trends, and competitor benchmarking matrices.
- **`user/`**: User interview transcripts, usability testing session notes, survey data, and feedback summaries.
- **`spikes/`**: Technical research spikes, proofs of concept (PoCs), library evaluations, and feasibility investigations.

## 💡 Guidelines

- Date all research documents to preserve chronological context.
- Explicitly state conclusions and actionable takeaways in each research document.
